Pick a category, draw a card, then Call Your Shot — Pocket it or Scratch. Pocketing a card grows your Pocket Score and keeps your Run alive. Scratching costs −5, and the card goes back in the pool.

8-Ball
The 8-Ball is the wild draw — it ignores your Poul Filter and pulls from any category. Use it when you want the pool to decide everything.

The deck is weighted toward Common. Legendary draws are rare by design — when one shows up, you'll know. The higher the rarity, the more memorable (and usually more expensive) the card.
Chalk
You start with 5 chalk cubes. Each redraw chalks up: 1 cube, −2 off your Pocket Score. The penalty is intentional — it makes you think twice before waving off a card. Use them wisely — chalk doesn't refill automatically.

Poul Filter
Not cheating — scoping. Before you draw, filter by cost, time of day, or duration. Cards outside your filter stay in the deck for later. The 8-Ball always ignores the filter.
The Bonus Challenge
Every card has a micro-dare — something extra to do while you're there. It's optional. But that's where the best stories come from.
City Packs
Your active pack is your card pool. Switch packs to explore a different city, or load one up before a trip. Tap Switch › on the home screen to change it.
