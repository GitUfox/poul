Chalk
You start with 5 chalk cubes. Each redraw chalks up: 1 cube, −2 off your Pocket Score. The penalty is intentional — it makes you think twice before waving off a card. You chalk up +1 cube on your first visit each day (max 5).

Poul Filter
Not cheating — scoping. Before you draw, filter by cost, time of day, or duration. Cards outside your filter stay in the deck for later. The 8-Ball always ignores the filter.
House Rules
Hosting the night? Build a custom rack — hand-pick cards and the table draws only from those. Set it inside the Poul Filter sheet. Switching packs clears it.
The Bonus Challenge
Every card has a micro-dare — something extra to do while you're there. It's optional. But that's where the best stories come from.
City Packs
Your active pack is your card pool. Switch packs to explore a different city, or load one up before a trip. Tap Switch City › on the home screen to change it.
